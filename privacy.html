CourtReady Privacy Policy

CourtReady is a personal training app for private use only.

This app connects to WHOOP to read recovery, sleep, strain, and workout data. All data is stored locally on your device and is never shared with any third party, transmitted to any server, or used for any purpose other than displaying your personal training information within the app.

No personal data is collected, stored remotely, or sold.

Contact: your own personal app — no external contact needed.
